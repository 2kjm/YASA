# Plans & billing

| Plan | Price | Support |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | community only, no ticket support |
| Pro | $29 / month | email support, triaged within 30 minutes during business hours |

Billing runs on Stripe. A customer is a paying customer when their email has an **active** Pro subscription.
Refunds and cancellations are done by a human in the billing team, never through support tickets.
