# Product overview

Acme Invoicing lets small businesses create invoices and send them to their clients.

- **Invoices**: each invoice has an id (`INV-1001`), a customer (company name), line items and a total.
- **Export**: any invoice can be downloaded as CSV from the invoice page (`Export` button). See *Invoice export*.
- **Invoice emails**: invoices are emailed to the client from `billing@acme-invoicing.example`.
- **Plans**: Free and Pro. See *Plans & billing*.

Signed-in users are identified by their email address everywhere (app, error tracker, billing).
